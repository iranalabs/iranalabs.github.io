Connect your way

Discover compatible ONVIF cameras or add an RTSP stream manually. Camera and network compatibility varies.

Build a useful view

Group saved cameras into dashboards and view up to nine streams at once, depending on device and network capacity.

Control supported cameras

Use pan, tilt, zoom, and presets when an ONVIF camera reports a compatible PTZ profile.

Your camera video stays between your device and camera.

iRana Cams does not use a developer-operated video relay. iCloud sync is off by default, and snapshot uploads require separate consent.
